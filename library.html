Neuro-Chemical Database

Chemical Classification(simplified):

  1. Excitatory: Stimulate neural activity (e.g., Glutamate).
  2. Inhibitory: Decrease neural activity (e.g., GABA).
  3. Modulatory: Adjust the "volume" of signals (e.g., Dopamine).
Transmitter Standard Function Substances
Dopamine Reward Circuitry & Motor Control (the "craving" one)
  • Heightened focus and euphoria
  • Increased motor activity (Nicotine/L-Dopa)
Serotonin Mood & Sleep Regulation (the "happiness" one)
  • Anxiety reduction
  • Emotional stabilization (SSRIs)
GABA Primary Neural Inhibition
  • Sedation and relaxation
  • Reduced neural firing (Alcohol)
Glutamate Learning & Memory (Excitatory)
  • Enhanced synaptic plasticity
  • Neural "noise" at high concentrations
Adenosine Sleep Pressure Homeostasis
  • Drowsiness (Blocked by Caffeine)
  • Lowered heart rate
