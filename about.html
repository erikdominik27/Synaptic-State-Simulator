The Idea

This simulator was made for people to visually and textually gather more knowledge about neurobiology and substances. By selecting various substances, users can visualize the theoretical shift in synaptic firing rates and understanding more about them with the proposed benefits and downsides.

How it Works

The "Neural Firing" on the homepage on the skull uses a jQuery-driven particle system. When a substance is selected, the script calculates a specific intensity variable showing the intensity of the "neuron firings" visually to understand the difference between stimulants and depressants while also showing the user textually information about it.:

  • Stimulants: Increase the frequency of neuron firings visually due to it stimulating the nervous system and the synapses.
  • Depressants: Trigger a delay in the neuron firing due to it suppressing the nervous system and synapses.
